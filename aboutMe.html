About Me

My name is Jordan DeMent and I have been an illustrator and concept artist for over five years, though I have been drawing all my life. My artistic style is semi-realistic with a stylized edge.

When I'm not drawing, I'm usually doing work in video game and software development or spending time in the woods.

Hiring me ensures that you will have an illustrator that works hard to realize your vision and bring your stories to life.
